Task: Build a read-only WBNB grid plan with 10 levels across a 15% band and 1000 USD notional. Include fees, break-even spacing and source block. Do not trade.

## Answer

### Price

`slot0()` returns token1 per token0, so the raw figure is WBNB per USDT and has
to be inverted. Both tokens carry 18 decimals, so no decimal adjustment applies.

```
sqrtPriceX96 / 2^96        = 0.0380445480
(sqrtPriceX96 / 2^96)^2    = 0.00144738763   WBNB per USDT
1 / that                   = 690.90          USDT per WBNB
```

Cross-checked against `tick`, an independent field in the same read:
`1.0001^(-65384) = 0.00144727456`, inverting to **690.95**. The two agree to
0.0078%, which is what confirms the derivation rather than just the arithmetic.

**WBNB = 690.90 USDT.**

### Fees

`fee() = 500`, denominated in hundredths of a basis point (1e-6):

```
500 / 1,000,000 = 0.05%   one way
                  0.10%   round trip
```

A grid earns only on completed round trips, so the round-trip figure is the one
that matters.

### The grid

Band is the mid price ±7.5%, giving the required 15% width. Ten levels at equal
arithmetic spacing:

```
low   = 690.90 x 0.925 = 639.0824 USDT
high  = 690.90 x 1.075 = 742.7174 USDT
step  = (high - low) / 9 = 11.5150 USDT = 1.6667% of mid
```

1000 USD notional split evenly is 100 USD per level.

### Break-even spacing

```
required spacing = round-trip fee = 0.10%
actual spacing   = 1.6667%
headroom         = 16.7x
```

The grid clears its own fees with room to spare. Per filled round trip on a
100 USD level: 1.6667 USD gross, 0.1000 USD fees, **1.5667 USD net**.

The break-even level count follows from the same figures: spacing would have to
fall below 0.10%, which across a 15% band means more than 150 levels, before
fees alone consume the edge.

## Assumptions

1. **Arithmetic spacing**, not geometric. Geometric spacing would give constant
   percentage steps and is the more common choice for a wide band; at 15% width
   the two differ by well under a tenth of a percent per level, so it does not
   change the break-even conclusion.
2. **Equal 100 USD allocation** per level. No weighting toward the band edges.
3. **Both tokens are 18 decimals**, confirmed on each token's BscScan page. This
   is what makes the decimal adjustment on the price a no-op; it would not hold
   for a USDC pair on other chains.
4. **The V3 0.05% pool is the venue.** Other fee tiers exist for this pair and
   would move the break-even figure proportionally.
5. **Fees only.** Gas is excluded, as is V3 price impact within a level, which
   is small at 100 USD against this pool's liquidity but not zero.
6. **A round trip is a fill on both sides.** Levels that fill once and never
   revert earn nothing and hold inventory.

## Risks

1. **Band exit is the dominant risk.** If WBNB leaves 639.08–742.72 the grid
   stops trading and holds one-sided inventory: above the band it is entirely in
   USDT and has stopped participating in the rise; below it is entirely in WBNB
   and is carrying the full drawdown. A 15% band is narrow for BNB, so this is a
   question of when, not whether.
2. **The price is a single-block snapshot.** It was read at block 119,264,446
   and is stale the moment the next block lands. A grid centred on a stale mid
   is asymmetric from the start.
3. **`slot0` is the spot price and is manipulable within a block.** Centring on
   the TWAP from `observe()` would be the more defensive choice for real capital.
